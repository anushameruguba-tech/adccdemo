Our story

A family business,
since 1990.

Julian Stone trained as a geneticist at Cardiff University. On an exchange in San Antonio, Texas, he worked in a drycleaning and laundry facility and saw how good garment care could be.

In 1990 he opened the first American Dry Cleaning Company shop near the American Embassy in Grosvenor Square. A second followed in Hampstead in 1991, and our own cleaning centre in 1994.

We were one of London's early online collection and delivery services. Today, run by Julian and Lucy Stone, our [220] people look after households and boutique hotels across [42] locations.
